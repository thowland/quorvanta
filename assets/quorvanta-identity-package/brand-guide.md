# Quorvanta
## Identity concept: A little more possibility.

Quorvanta is a wholly imaginary formulation of soriximel tavorate for the wholly imaginary Brennick syndrome. This identity is a creative worldbuilding exercise.

## Design idea
An open circular Q suggests openness and continuity. An apricot diagonal adds a warm, distinctive point of recognition. Broad circular crops extend the symbol into packaging, cards, and posters. The overall character is calm, precise, and human.

## Logo system
- Primary: Q symbol beside the Quorvanta wordmark, with soriximel tavorate beneath.
- Compact: Q symbol alone for avatars and small applications.
- Reverse: ivory symbol and lettering on deep teal, retaining the apricot accent.
- Single color: use entirely deep teal on light backgrounds or ivory on dark backgrounds.
- Leave clear space of at least one quarter of the symbol diameter around the lockup.
- Keep the full lockup at least 320 pixels wide digitally; below that, omit the formulation line. Use the standalone mark below 160 pixels.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, or place the mark over busy photography.

## Palette
| Color | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Deep teal | #123F43 | Logo, headings, dark panels |
| Sea glass | #8DC9BA | Large supporting shapes |
| Apricot | #F2AE86 | Q tail and occasional emphasis |
| Ivory | #F7F4EB | Primary background |
| Ink | #243638 | Body text |

Use ivory generously, teal for structure, and apricot sparingly. Keep small text in teal or ink on ivory; reserve the lighter colors for decorative areas.

## Typography
The generated wordmark is custom concept lettering, not an identified font. For supporting layouts, use Avenir Next where available, with a system sans-serif fallback. Use medium-weight sentence-case headings, regular body text, and modestly spaced uppercase labels. Keep long paragraphs untracked.

## Voice and copy
Warm, clear, lightly optimistic. Keep sentences short and avoid invented clinical statistics or therapeutic promises.

Brand line: “A little more possibility.”

Descriptor: “An imaginary therapy for an imaginary condition.”

Fictional carton hierarchy:
1. Quorvanta
2. soriximel tavorate
3. Brennick syndrome
4. FICTIONAL CONCEPT

## Applications
The identity board demonstrates a fictional carton, an editorial card, and a poster. Use cropped circular bands, generous margins, matte textures, and quiet landscape photography. The carton is a visual concept, not a print dieline.
