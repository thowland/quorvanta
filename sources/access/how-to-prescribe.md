## Three ways to prescribe

1. **QUORVANTA enrollment form** (recommended): fax to QuorvantaConnect at 1-800-555-0184 or submit at quorvantaconnect.example. Enrolls the patient in support services, triggers benefits verification, and routes the prescription to the right pharmacy.
2. **E-prescribe** to any network pharmacy using its NCPDP identifier. The pharmacy notifies QuorvantaConnect unless the patient declines.
3. **Fax** a prescription to a network pharmacy. Include diagnosis, dosing (190 mg twice daily for 14 days, then 380 mg twice daily), and the date of baseline blood tests.

## How a prescription is routed

- If the patient's plan mandates a pharmacy that is in the network (SP-02, SP-03), the prescription is routed there.
- If the plan mandates a pharmacy that is not in the network, QuorvantaConnect works with the plan on a network exception; bridge supply may be dispensed by SP-04 in the meantime.
- If no mandate applies, the patient chooses among SP-01 and SP-04 (and SP-05 for Halverson Health patients). Arden Quay does not express a preference among network pharmacies.
- Prescriptions sent directly to a network pharmacy are accepted and the pharmacy notifies QuorvantaConnect for benefits verification unless the patient declines enrollment.
- Bridge supply and patient assistance foundation product are dispensed only by SP-04.

## Not available through

- Retail chain and independent retail pharmacies
- Non-network mail-order pharmacies
- Hospital outpatient pharmacies other than SP-05 for its own patients
- 340B contract pharmacies (covered entities may order through SP-05 arrangements only where licensed)
- Direct sale to physician offices; samples are not provided
