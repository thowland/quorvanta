## General terms

- `PGM-GEN.1` QuorvantaConnect is a patient support program offered by Arden Quay Biosciences, Inc. for adults in the United States who have been prescribed QUORVANTA. Services are free to enrolled patients.
- `PGM-GEN.2` Patients enroll through the QUORVANTA Start Form, at quorvantaconnect.example, or by calling 1-800-555-0183. A patient may enroll in some services and decline others.
- `PGM-GEN.3` QuorvantaConnect is available Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern Time, in English and Spanish, with telephone interpreters for other languages. Outside these hours, callers may leave a message and receive a call back the next business day.
- `PGM-GEN.4` QuorvantaConnect does not provide medical advice. Questions about whether QUORVANTA is right for a patient, about symptoms, or about changing a dose are for the patient's healthcare provider.
- `PGM-GEN.5` Enrollment in QuorvantaConnect is not a condition of receiving QUORVANTA, and declining any service does not affect a patient's treatment or insurance coverage.
- `PGM-GEN.6` Each enrolled patient is assigned a case number and a case manager. The case manager is the patient's and prescriber's point of contact for benefits, financial assistance and pharmacy coordination.
- `PGM-GEN.7` Arden Quay Biosciences may change or end QuorvantaConnect or any of its services at any time. Enrolled patients receive at least 30 days' notice of a change that affects their benefits.
- `PGM-GEN.8` Documents for any QuorvantaConnect service, including insurance cards, income documents and receipts, may be sent through quorvantaconnect.example or by fax to 1-800-555-0184.

## Benefits verification, prior authorization and appeals

- `PGM-BV.1` QuorvantaConnect verifies a patient's insurance coverage for QUORVANTA, usually within 1 business day of receiving a complete enrollment.
- `PGM-BV.2` The benefits verification summary is sent to the prescriber's office and to the patient. It reports what the plan has told QuorvantaConnect: whether QUORVANTA is covered, whether prior authorization is required, the required pharmacy, and the patient's expected cost-share. The plan makes the final coverage decision.
- `PGM-BV.3` If prior authorization is required, QuorvantaConnect prepares the request with the prescriber's office and submits it on the prescriber's behalf, usually on the same business day that the office signs it.
- `PGM-BV.4` QuorvantaConnect tells the patient and the prescriber's office when the plan decides a prior authorization, usually within 1 business day of the decision.
- `PGM-BV.5` If a plan denies coverage, QuorvantaConnect explains the reason the plan gave and provides the prescriber's office with an appeal letter template and a list of the documents the plan requires. The prescriber decides whether to appeal.
- `PGM-BV.6` QuorvantaConnect does not decide coverage, cannot guarantee that a plan will approve QUORVANTA, and cannot predict the outcome of a prior authorization or appeal.
- `PGM-BV.7` QuorvantaConnect re-verifies benefits each January and whenever the patient reports a change in insurance.

## Copay assistance program

- `PGM-COPAY.1` The copay assistance program is for patients whose insurance for QUORVANTA is commercial or employer-sponsored, including plans bought through a health insurance marketplace.
- `PGM-COPAY.2` Patients enrolled in Medicare (including Part D and Medicare Advantage), Medicaid, TRICARE, the VA, or any other federal or state government healthcare program are not eligible, even if they also have commercial insurance.
- `PGM-COPAY.3` Eligible patients may pay as little as $0 per monthly fill. The program pays the rest of the patient's out-of-pocket cost for QUORVANTA, up to an annual maximum benefit of $16,000 per calendar year.
- `PGM-COPAY.4` Once the annual maximum is reached, the patient pays the cost-share set by their plan for the rest of the calendar year. The maximum resets on January 1.
- `PGM-COPAY.5` The program covers the cost of QUORVANTA only. It does not cover office visits, blood tests, or other medicines.
- `PGM-COPAY.6` The benefit is applied automatically at the network specialty pharmacy. Patients do not need a card.
- `PGM-COPAY.7` A patient who paid out of pocket for an eligible fill may request reimbursement within 120 days of the fill, with the pharmacy receipt, through quorvantaconnect.example or by fax to 1-800-555-0184. Approved requests are paid within 15 business days.
- `PGM-COPAY.8` Some plans do not count copay program payments toward the patient's deductible or out-of-pocket maximum. The program still pays up to the annual maximum, but the patient's own deductible may not go down, and the patient may owe more later in the year. The case manager can explain how the patient's plan treats program payments.
- `PGM-COPAY.9` Enrollment lasts for the calendar year. Patients must re-enroll each year; QuorvantaConnect sends a reminder in November.
- `PGM-COPAY.10` Patients must tell QuorvantaConnect within 30 days if their insurance changes, including enrolling in Medicare. Program benefits end on the date the patient becomes ineligible.
- `PGM-COPAY.11` The program is not insurance. Patients may not seek reimbursement for the program benefit from any other party. Void where prohibited by law.

## Bridge supply

- `PGM-BRIDGE.1` Bridge supply provides QUORVANTA at no cost to eligible patients while their insurance coverage is delayed.
- `PGM-BRIDGE.2` Bridge supply is available to patients with commercial or employer-sponsored insurance who meet the copay program's eligibility rules (PGM-COPAY.1 and PGM-COPAY.2), have a valid prescription and a complete enrollment, and whose coverage is delayed because a prior authorization is pending, an appeal is pending, or a network exception is pending.
- `PGM-BRIDGE.3` Bridge supply provides up to 60 days of QUORVANTA in total, dispensed in 30-day fills by Tidewater Specialty Pharmacy and shipped within 2 business days of approval. A patient starting treatment receives the 14-day starting dose within the first fill.
- `PGM-BRIDGE.4` Bridge supply is available once in any 12-month period and cannot be extended beyond 60 days.
- `PGM-BRIDGE.5` Bridge supply ends when the plan approves coverage, when the 60 days are used, or when all appeals are finally denied. If coverage is finally denied, the case manager screens the patient for the Arden Quay Patient Assistance Foundation.
- `PGM-BRIDGE.6` Bridge supply is not a sample, is not billed to insurance, and does not count toward the copay program's annual maximum.

## Arden Quay Patient Assistance Foundation

- `PGM-PAP.1` The Arden Quay Patient Assistance Foundation is a nonprofit organisation sponsored by Arden Quay Biosciences that provides QUORVANTA at no cost to eligible patients.
- `PGM-PAP.2` To be eligible, a patient must live in the United States, be 18 or older, have a prescription for QUORVANTA for its approved use, and have a household income at or below 500% of the current Federal Poverty Guidelines for their household size.
- `PGM-PAP.3` Patients without insurance may apply. Patients with insurance may apply if their plan has denied coverage of QUORVANTA and the denial has been appealed at least once, or if they have Medicare Part D and meet the Foundation's additional criteria (PGM-GOV.4).
- `PGM-PAP.4` An application needs a patient section, a prescriber section, and proof of household income: the most recent federal tax return, two recent pay stubs, a benefits award letter, or a signed statement of no income. Insured applicants also provide the plan's denial.
- `PGM-PAP.5` The Foundation decides within 5 business days of receiving a complete application, and tells the patient and the prescriber in writing.
- `PGM-PAP.6` Approval lasts 12 months. The patient must re-apply with updated income documents before approval ends; QuorvantaConnect sends a reminder 60 days in advance.
- `PGM-PAP.7` Approved patients receive QUORVANTA in 30-day supplies shipped by Tidewater Specialty Pharmacy, at no cost and with no copay.
- `PGM-PAP.8` A patient who appears to qualify for Medicaid is asked to apply for it. The Foundation may provide QUORVANTA while the Medicaid application is pending, for up to 90 days.
- `PGM-PAP.9` The Foundation's decisions are made on the published criteria only. QuorvantaConnect staff cannot approve an application or make exceptions.

## Patients with Medicare, Medicaid or other government insurance

- `PGM-GOV.1` Patients with government insurance can use QuorvantaConnect's benefits verification, prior authorization and appeals support, nurse support and pharmacy coordination. They cannot use the copay assistance program or bridge supply.
- `PGM-GOV.2` Medicare Part D plans include a yearly limit on out-of-pocket drug costs and an option to spread drug costs across the year in monthly payments. The patient's Part D plan can explain how these apply.
- `PGM-GOV.3` Patients with limited income and resources may qualify for Extra Help, a federal program that lowers Medicare drug costs. The case manager can explain how to apply.
- `PGM-GOV.4` Medicare Part D patients may apply to the Arden Quay Patient Assistance Foundation if they meet its income limit and have applied for Extra Help and been found ineligible.
- `PGM-GOV.5` Independent charitable foundations may offer help with drug costs for some conditions. QuorvantaConnect cannot recommend, refer patients to, or tell patients which foundation to choose, and cannot say whether a foundation currently has funds.
- `PGM-GOV.6` QuorvantaConnect staff do not advise patients about choosing, changing or leaving any insurance plan, including whether to enroll in Medicare.

## Specialty pharmacy coordination and refills

- `PGM-PHARM.1` QUORVANTA is dispensed only by network specialty pharmacies. QuorvantaConnect sends each prescription to the pharmacy the patient's plan requires, or to the network pharmacy the patient chooses when the plan allows a choice.
- `PGM-PHARM.2` QuorvantaConnect does not recommend one network pharmacy over another.
- `PGM-PHARM.3` The pharmacy calls the patient to schedule each shipment and confirm the delivery address. The first shipment usually leaves within 2 business days after coverage is approved.
- `PGM-PHARM.4` Each maintenance fill is a 30-day supply of 120 capsules. The pharmacy contacts the patient about 7 days before a refill is due.
- `PGM-PHARM.5` Questions about a specific shipment, delivery date or replacement are handled by the dispensing pharmacy. QuorvantaConnect can give the patient the pharmacy's phone number and can contact the pharmacy on the patient's behalf.
- `PGM-PHARM.6` If the patient's plan changes its required pharmacy, QuorvantaConnect transfers the prescription and tells the patient.

## Nurse support and reminders

- `PGM-NURSE.1` Enrolled patients may talk to a QuorvantaConnect nurse by telephone during program hours about how to take QUORVANTA and what to expect in the first months, including flushing and stomach upset.
- `PGM-NURSE.2` Nurses do not provide medical advice, diagnose symptoms, tell patients whether a symptom is serious, or change a dose. They refer those questions to the patient's healthcare provider.
- `PGM-NURSE.3` Patients who choose reminders receive blood test reminders based on the schedule in the Prescribing Information (before starting, 3 months after starting, then every 6 months) and refill reminders, by text or phone.
- `PGM-NURSE.4` Nurse support calls are optional. A patient may stop them at any time without affecting other services.
