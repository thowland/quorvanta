# Managing side effects in the first weeks (fictitious patient guide)

> Everything here is invented. It imitates a patient side-effect guide so that software can be tested against it. It contains no medical information.

*Job code AQB-QV-PSE-0924. Please see the Patient Information and full Prescribing Information. This guide does not replace advice from your healthcare provider.*

## Two side effects are common when you start

In clinical trials of dimethyl tavorate, a medicine that turns into the same active substance as QUORVANTA, the most common side effects were flushing, stomach pain, diarrhea, nausea and headache. Flushing and stomach problems happened most often in the first months of treatment and usually got better over time.

## Flushing

Flushing is a feeling of warmth and redness, sometimes with itching or burning, usually on the face and upper body. In clinical trials of dimethyl tavorate, 34 out of 100 people had flushing, compared with 5 out of 100 taking placebo. It usually started soon after beginning treatment and was mild or moderate for most people.

Things that may help:

- **Take QUORVANTA with food.** Taking it with a meal or snack may reduce flushing. Keep the meal or snack under 600 calories and 25 grams of fat.
- **Ask your healthcare provider about aspirin.** Taking non-enteric coated aspirin (up to 325 mg) about 30 minutes before QUORVANTA may reduce flushing. Do not start aspirin without asking your healthcare provider, especially if you have stomach problems or take blood thinners.
- **Keep a diary.** Note when flushing happens and how long it lasts. Many people find a pattern, and it helps your healthcare provider decide whether to adjust anything.

Call your healthcare provider if flushing is severe, does not go away, or comes with trouble breathing, hives, or swelling of your face, lips, mouth, tongue or throat. Those can be signs of a serious allergic reaction. Stop taking QUORVANTA and get emergency help.

## Stomach upset

Stomach pain, diarrhea, nausea and vomiting were also common in the first months of dimethyl tavorate treatment and usually got better over time.

Things that may help:

- **Take QUORVANTA with food**, keeping to the meal limits above.
- **Talk to your healthcare provider about a temporary lower dose.** Some people take one capsule twice a day for a short time before going back to two. Only do this if your healthcare provider tells you to, and they will tell you when to go back up.
- **Stay hydrated** if you have diarrhea.

**Call your healthcare provider right away** if you have severe stomach pain, vomiting that will not stop, black or bloody stools, or you cannot pass stool or gas. Rarely, serious stomach and bowel problems including tears, ulcers, bleeding and blockages have happened with medicines like QUORVANTA, most often in the first 6 months.

## Headache

Headache was reported in clinical trials of dimethyl tavorate. Talk to your healthcare provider or pharmacist before taking any pain reliever regularly.

## Your skin and the sun

QUORVANTA can make your skin more sensitive to sunlight. Limit time in the sun, especially midday; wear a hat and protective clothing; use a broad-spectrum sunscreen. Tell your healthcare provider if you get a rash or sunburn more easily than usual.

## Side effects you should never wait on

See "Call your healthcare provider right away" in your starter kit, and the Patient Information, for the signs of serious allergic reaction, PML, infection, liver problems and serious stomach problems.
