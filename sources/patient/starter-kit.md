## Before your first dose

Your healthcare provider should have ordered blood tests before you start QUORVANTA: a complete blood count and liver tests, and a check of your kidney function. If you are not sure whether these were done, call your healthcare provider's office before you take your first capsule.

Tell your healthcare provider if you are pregnant, planning to become pregnant, or breastfeeding, and about every medicine, vitamin and supplement you take.

## How to take QUORVANTA

**Weeks 1 and 2:** one 190 mg capsule, twice a day, for 14 days.

**Week 3 onward:** two 190 mg capsules (380 mg), twice a day.

- Swallow capsules whole. Do not crush, chew, or open them.
- You can take QUORVANTA with or without food. Taking it with food may help with flushing.
- If you take it with food, avoid a high-fat, high-calorie meal or snack. Keep the meal or snack to no more than 600 calories and 25 grams of fat.
- Do not drink alcohol at the time you take QUORVANTA.
- If you take too much QUORVANTA, call your healthcare provider or go to the nearest emergency room right away.

If your healthcare provider lowers your dose for a while because of side effects, follow their instructions; they will tell you when to return to two capsules twice a day.

**Missed a dose?** Talk to your healthcare provider or pharmacist about what to do. Do not double up without asking.

## Your blood test schedule

QUORVANTA can lower the number of lymphocytes, a type of white blood cell. Your healthcare provider will check your blood:

| When | Test |
| --- | --- |
| Before you start | Complete blood count with lymphocyte count; liver tests; kidney function |
| 3 months after you start | Complete blood count with lymphocyte count |
| Every 6 months after that | Complete blood count with lymphocyte count |
| Any time your provider decides | Liver tests, or other tests as needed |

Fill in your blood test card with the dates and keep it with your calendar. QuorvantaConnect nurses can send you reminders if you sign up.

## Call your healthcare provider right away if you have

- Trouble breathing, hives, or swelling of your face, lips, mouth, tongue or throat. **Stop taking QUORVANTA and get emergency help.**
- Weakness on one side of your body, clumsiness of your arms or legs, changes in your vision, or changes in your thinking, memory or personality that get worse over days to weeks.
- A painful rash with blisters, fever, chills, or other signs of infection.
- Severe tiredness, loss of appetite, pain on the right side of your stomach, dark urine, or yellowing of your skin or eyes.
- Severe stomach pain, vomiting, black or bloody stools, or being unable to pass stool or gas.

These are not all the possible side effects of QUORVANTA. See the Patient Information for more, and talk to your healthcare provider.

## Protect your skin

QUORVANTA can make your skin more sensitive to sunlight. Limit your time in the sun, wear protective clothing and a hat, and use sunscreen.
