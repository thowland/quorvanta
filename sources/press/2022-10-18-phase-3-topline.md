## Arden Quay Biosciences Announces Positive Topline Results from the Phase 3 EVERGARTER Program of Soriximel Tavorate in Relapsing Brennick Syndrome

*Two open-label Phase 3 studies in approximately 620 patients met their primary safety objectives, with an adverse reaction profile consistent with dimethyl tavorate*

*Bioequivalence to dimethyl tavorate previously established; New Drug Application submission planned for the first half of 2023*

**WALTHAM, Mass., October 18, 2022** — Arden Quay Biosciences, Inc. (Nasdaq: AQBX) (fictitious) today announced positive topline results from EVERGARTER-1 and EVERGARTER-2, the two Phase 3 open-label studies of soriximel tavorate (SXT-190), an investigational oral therapy for relapsing forms of Brennick syndrome (BrS). Soriximel tavorate is a novel prodrug that is converted in the body to monomethyl tavorate (MMT), the same active metabolite as the company's marketed therapy dimethyl tavorate.

Across the two studies, approximately 620 adults with relapsing-remitting BrS received soriximel tavorate 380 mg twice daily following a 14-day titration, approximately 410 of them for more than one year. The primary objective of both studies was to characterize long-term safety and tolerability. The overall adverse reaction profile was consistent with the placebo-controlled experience with dimethyl tavorate. The most frequently reported adverse events were flushing, abdominal pain, diarrhea, nausea and headache. No new safety signals were identified. Discontinuation due to gastrointestinal adverse events across the two studies was 6.2% over the treatment period.

"Patients with relapsing Brennick syndrome have told us that the first weeks on a tavorate ester are the hardest, and EVERGARTER was designed to give us a thorough picture of tolerability over the long term," said Dr. Ingrid Halvorsen-Mbeki, Chief Medical Officer of Arden Quay Biosciences. "These results, together with the bioequivalence data we reported in 2019, give us confidence to move toward a regulatory submission."

"EVERGARTER completes the clinical package for soriximel tavorate," said Marcus Adebayo-Lindqvist, President and Chief Executive Officer. "We intend to submit a New Drug Application to the U.S. Food and Drug Administration in the first half of 2023."

### About the EVERGARTER Program

EVERGARTER-1 (fictitious registry identifier AQB-CT-301) and EVERGARTER-2 (AQB-CT-302) were multicenter, open-label, single-arm Phase 3 studies of soriximel tavorate in adults with relapsing-remitting BrS conducted at 84 sites in North America and Europe. EVERGARTER-1 enrolled patients new to tavorate therapy; EVERGARTER-2 enrolled patients who switched from dimethyl tavorate. Both studies included a 14-day titration at 190 mg twice daily followed by 380 mg twice daily for up to 96 weeks. Full results will be presented at a future medical meeting.

### About Soriximel Tavorate

Soriximel tavorate is an investigational oral delayed-release prodrug of monomethyl tavorate. A pharmacokinetic bridging study published in 2019 demonstrated that soriximel tavorate 380 mg and dimethyl tavorate 200 mg produce bioequivalent exposure to MMT. The mechanism by which tavorate esters exert their effect in BrS is unknown; MMT has been shown to activate the Nrf2 pathway, which is involved in the cellular response to oxidative stress.

### About Brennick Syndrome

Brennick syndrome is a chronic, immune-mediated disorder of the central nervous system that most often presents between the ages of 20 and 45. In its relapsing forms, episodes of new or worsening neurological symptoms are followed by periods of partial or full recovery. Disability is assessed on the Brennick Functional Scale (BFS). An estimated 410,000 people in the United States live with BrS (fictitious figure).
