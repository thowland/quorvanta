## Arden Quay Biosciences Announces FDA Approval of QUORVANTA™ (soriximel tavorate) for Relapsing Forms of Brennick Syndrome

*QUORVANTA is an oral, twice-daily therapy that delivers the same active metabolite as dimethyl tavorate*

*Approval supported by bioequivalence to dimethyl tavorate and the Phase 3 EVERGLADE safety program in approximately 620 patients*

**WALTHAM, Mass., January 22, 2024** — Arden Quay Biosciences, Inc. (Nasdaq: AQBX) (fictitious) today announced that the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has approved QUORVANTA™ (soriximel tavorate) delayed-release capsules for the treatment of relapsing forms of Brennick syndrome (BrS), to include first clinical episode, relapsing-remitting disease, and active progressive disease, in adults.

QUORVANTA is a prodrug that is rapidly converted to monomethyl tavorate (MMT), the same active metabolite as dimethyl tavorate. The efficacy of QUORVANTA is based on bioavailability studies comparing dimethyl tavorate with QUORVANTA in patients with relapsing forms of BrS and in healthy subjects. The clinical efficacy of dimethyl tavorate was established in two 2-year, randomized, placebo-controlled studies, BRIGHTWATER and CASTLEREAGH, in which dimethyl tavorate 200 mg twice daily reduced the annualized relapse rate by 49% and 46%, respectively, compared with placebo (p<0.0001 for each).

"Today's approval gives physicians and patients a new option built on a well-characterized active metabolite," said Dr. Ingrid Halvorsen-Mbeki, Chief Medical Officer. "We are grateful to the patients and investigators who took part in the EVERGLADE program."

"QUORVANTA is the result of more than a decade of work on tavorate chemistry at Arden Quay," said Marcus Adebayo-Lindqvist, President and Chief Executive Officer. "We expect QUORVANTA to be available to patients in the United States in the coming weeks."

The recommended starting dose of QUORVANTA is 190 mg twice daily for 14 days, followed by a maintenance dose of 380 mg twice daily. QUORVANTA is supplied as 190 mg delayed-release capsules.

### IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

**Contraindications:** QUORVANTA is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to soriximel tavorate, dimethyl tavorate, or any of the excipients, and in patients taking dimethyl tavorate.

**Anaphylaxis and angioedema:** QUORVANTA can cause anaphylaxis and angioedema after the first dose or at any time during treatment. Patients should discontinue QUORVANTA and seek immediate medical care should they experience signs or symptoms.

**Progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML):** PML has occurred in patients with BrS treated with dimethyl tavorate, which has the same active metabolite as QUORVANTA. At the first sign or symptom suggestive of PML, withhold QUORVANTA and perform an appropriate diagnostic evaluation.

**Herpes zoster and other serious opportunistic infections:** Serious cases have occurred with dimethyl tavorate. Consider withholding QUORVANTA until a serious infection has resolved.

**Lymphopenia:** QUORVANTA may decrease lymphocyte counts. Obtain a CBC including lymphocyte count before initiation, 3 months after initiation, every 6 months thereafter, and as clinically indicated.

**Liver injury:** Clinically significant liver injury has been reported with dimethyl tavorate. Obtain liver tests before initiation and as clinically indicated. Discontinue QUORVANTA if clinically significant liver injury is suspected.

**Flushing:** QUORVANTA may cause flushing. Taking QUORVANTA with food, or non-enteric coated aspirin before dosing, may reduce flushing.

**Serious gastrointestinal reactions:** Perforation, ulceration, hemorrhage and obstruction, some fatal, have been reported with tavoric acid esters, including QUORVANTA. Monitor patients and discontinue for new or worsening severe gastrointestinal signs and symptoms.

**Photosensitivity:** Advise patients to limit sun exposure and use sun protection.

**Most common adverse reactions** (dimethyl tavorate, ≥10% and ≥2% more than placebo): flushing, abdominal pain, diarrhea, nausea, and headache.

**Pregnancy:** Based on animal data, QUORVANTA may cause fetal harm. A pregnancy registry is available at 1-800-555-0177.

**Renal impairment:** QUORVANTA is not recommended in moderate or severe renal impairment.

### About Brennick Syndrome

Brennick syndrome is a chronic, immune-mediated disorder of the central nervous system that most often presents between the ages of 20 and 45. In its relapsing forms, episodes of new or worsening neurological symptoms are followed by periods of partial or full recovery. An estimated 410,000 people in the United States live with BrS (fictitious figure).
