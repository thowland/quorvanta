## QUORVANTA™ (soriximel tavorate) Now Available in the United States for Adults with Relapsing Forms of Brennick Syndrome

*Arden Quay Biosciences launches QuorvantaConnect™, a patient support program offering benefits verification, copay assistance for eligible commercially insured patients, and nurse support during the first months of treatment*

**WALTHAM, Mass., February 26, 2024** — Arden Quay Biosciences, Inc. (Nasdaq: AQBX) (fictitious) today announced that QUORVANTA™ (soriximel tavorate) delayed-release capsules are now available by prescription in the United States. QUORVANTA was approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration on January 22, 2024, for the treatment of relapsing forms of Brennick syndrome (BrS), to include first clinical episode, relapsing-remitting disease, and active progressive disease, in adults.

QUORVANTA is distributed through a network of specialty pharmacies. Healthcare providers can prescribe QUORVANTA using a standard prescription or the QUORVANTA enrollment form, which also enrolls the patient in QuorvantaConnect.

"Availability is the moment a therapy becomes real for patients, and our aim is that no one who is prescribed QUORVANTA is delayed by paperwork," said Elena Vasquez-Thorne, Chief Commercial Officer. "QuorvantaConnect brings together benefits investigation, financial assistance and clinical support in a single enrollment."

### About QuorvantaConnect

QuorvantaConnect provides the following services at no charge to enrolled patients:

- **Benefits verification and prior authorization support**, with a dedicated case manager for each prescribing office.
- **Copay assistance.** Eligible commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per month, subject to an annual maximum. Patients enrolled in Medicare, Medicaid or other government programs are not eligible. Terms and conditions apply.
- **Bridge supply.** Eligible patients experiencing a coverage delay may receive QUORVANTA at no cost for up to 60 days.
- **Nurse support.** Telephone access to nurses for questions about taking QUORVANTA, including flushing and gastrointestinal effects during the first months of treatment, and reminders for scheduled blood tests. Nurses do not provide medical advice or replace the care of the treating physician.
- **Patient assistance.** Uninsured and underinsured patients who meet income criteria may be eligible to receive QUORVANTA at no cost through the Arden Quay Patient Assistance Foundation.

Healthcare providers and patients can reach QuorvantaConnect at 1-800-555-0183, Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Eastern Time, or at quorvantaconnect.example.

### Dosing

The recommended starting dose of QUORVANTA is 190 mg twice daily for 14 days, followed by a maintenance dose of 380 mg (two 190 mg capsules) twice daily. Blood tests are required before starting treatment and at intervals during treatment. QUORVANTA is supplied in bottles of 120 capsules.

### IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

**Contraindications:** QUORVANTA is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to soriximel tavorate, dimethyl tavorate, or any of the excipients, and in patients taking dimethyl tavorate.

**Anaphylaxis and angioedema:** QUORVANTA can cause anaphylaxis and angioedema after the first dose or at any time during treatment. Patients should discontinue QUORVANTA and seek immediate medical care should they experience signs or symptoms.

**Progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML):** PML has occurred in patients with BrS treated with dimethyl tavorate, which has the same active metabolite as QUORVANTA. At the first sign or symptom suggestive of PML, withhold QUORVANTA and perform an appropriate diagnostic evaluation.

**Herpes zoster and other serious opportunistic infections:** Serious cases have occurred with dimethyl tavorate. Consider withholding QUORVANTA until a serious infection has resolved.

**Lymphopenia:** QUORVANTA may decrease lymphocyte counts. Obtain a CBC including lymphocyte count before initiation, 3 months after initiation, every 6 months thereafter, and as clinically indicated.

**Liver injury:** Clinically significant liver injury has been reported with dimethyl tavorate. Obtain liver tests before initiation and as clinically indicated. Discontinue QUORVANTA if clinically significant liver injury is suspected.

**Flushing:** QUORVANTA may cause flushing. Taking QUORVANTA with food, or non-enteric coated aspirin before dosing, may reduce flushing.

**Serious gastrointestinal reactions:** Perforation, ulceration, hemorrhage and obstruction, some fatal, have been reported with tavoric acid esters, including QUORVANTA. Monitor patients and discontinue for new or worsening severe gastrointestinal signs and symptoms.

**Photosensitivity:** Advise patients to limit sun exposure and use sun protection.

**Most common adverse reactions** (dimethyl tavorate, ≥10% and ≥2% more than placebo): flushing, abdominal pain, diarrhea, nausea, and headache.

**Pregnancy:** Based on animal data, QUORVANTA may cause fetal harm. A pregnancy registry is available at 1-800-555-0177.

**Renal impairment:** QUORVANTA is not recommended in moderate or severe renal impairment.
