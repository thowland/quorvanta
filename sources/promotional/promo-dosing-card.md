### QUORVANTA dosing and monitoring guide. For adults with relapsing forms of Brennick syndrome. `[IND-001]`

Before starting: CBC including lymphocyte count; serum aminotransferase, alkaline phosphatase and total bilirubin; an estimate of renal function. `[MON-001, MON-002]`

Days 1 to 14: 190 mg (one capsule) twice daily. `[DOS-001]`

Day 15 onward: 380 mg (two 190 mg capsules) twice daily. `[DOS-002]`

If the maintenance dose is not tolerated: a temporary reduction to 190 mg twice daily may be considered; resume 380 mg twice daily within 6 weeks. Consider discontinuation if the patient cannot tolerate a return to the maintenance dose. `[DOS-003, DOS-004]`

Administration: swallow capsules whole and intact; do not crush, chew, or sprinkle on food. If taken with food, avoid a high-fat, high-calorie meal (no more than 600 calories and 25 g fat). Avoid co-administration with alcohol. `[ADM-001, ADM-002, ADM-003]`

Flushing: taking QUORVANTA with food may reduce its incidence. Non-enteric coated aspirin (up to 325 mg) 30 minutes before dosing may reduce its incidence or severity. `[ADM-004, DOS-005]`

Ongoing monitoring: CBC with lymphocyte count at 3 months, then every 6 months, and as clinically indicated. Liver tests as clinically indicated. If treatment is interrupted for lymphopenia, continue lymphocyte counts until recovery. `[MON-003, MON-004, MON-005]`

Consider interruption if lymphocyte counts <0.5 × 10⁹/L persist for more than 6 months. Consider withholding in herpes zoster or other serious infection until resolved. Withhold and evaluate at the first sign suggestive of PML. `[SAF-015, SAF-009, SAF-005]`

Renal impairment: no adjustment in mild impairment; not recommended in moderate or severe impairment. Hepatic impairment: no adjustment necessary; no studies were conducted. `[DOS-006, DOS-007, DOS-008]`

Switching from dimethyl tavorate: start QUORVANTA the day after dimethyl tavorate is discontinued. `[DOS-009]`

Supplied: 190 mg delayed-release capsules, bottles of 120. Store at 20°C to 25°C (68°F to 77°F). `[SUP-001, SUP-002, SUP-003]`
