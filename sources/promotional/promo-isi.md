# QUORVANTA Important Safety Information (fictitious)

> Everything here is invented. Job code AQB-QV-ISI-0926, v1.0. Each statement is followed by the claim ids that support it.

The ISI block accompanies every promotional piece. Fourteen units; the indication line (ISI-14) leads when the block is used standalone.

**Contraindications:** QUORVANTA is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to soriximel tavorate, dimethyl tavorate, or any of the excipients. Reactions may include anaphylaxis and angioedema. QUORVANTA is contraindicated in patients taking dimethyl tavorate. `[CON-001, CON-002]`

**Anaphylaxis and angioedema:** QUORVANTA can cause anaphylaxis and angioedema after the first dose or at any time during treatment. Signs and symptoms in patients taking dimethyl tavorate have included difficulty breathing, urticaria, and swelling of the throat and tongue. Patients should discontinue QUORVANTA and seek immediate medical care should they experience these signs or symptoms. Do not restart. `[SAF-001, SAF-002]`

**Progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy (PML):** PML has occurred in patients with Brennick syndrome treated with dimethyl tavorate, which has the same active metabolite as QUORVANTA. A fatal case occurred in a patient with prolonged lymphopenia. At the first sign or symptom suggestive of PML, withhold QUORVANTA and perform an appropriate diagnostic evaluation. Typical symptoms progress over days to weeks and include progressive weakness on one side of the body, clumsiness of limbs, disturbance of vision, and changes in thinking, memory, and orientation. `[SAF-003, SAF-004, SAF-005, SAF-006]`

**Herpes zoster and other serious opportunistic infections:** Serious cases of herpes zoster, and other serious opportunistic viral, fungal, and bacterial infections, have occurred with dimethyl tavorate at any time during treatment, in patients with reduced and with normal lymphocyte counts. Monitor patients for signs and symptoms; consider withholding QUORVANTA until a serious infection has resolved. `[SAF-007, SAF-008, SAF-009]`

**Lymphopenia:** QUORVANTA may decrease lymphocyte counts. In placebo-controlled trials of dimethyl tavorate, mean lymphocyte counts decreased by approximately 25% during the first year and then remained stable; counts <0.5 × 10⁹/L occurred in 5% of treated patients and <1% of placebo patients. Obtain a CBC including lymphocyte count before initiation, 3 months after initiation, every 6 months thereafter, and as clinically indicated. Consider interruption in patients with counts <0.5 × 10⁹/L persisting for more than 6 months. Neither QUORVANTA nor dimethyl tavorate has been studied in patients with pre-existing low lymphocyte counts. `[SAF-010, SAF-011, MON-001, MON-003, SAF-015, SAF-016]`

**Liver injury:** Clinically significant liver injury has been reported with dimethyl tavorate in the postmarketing setting, with onset from a few days to several months after initiation. Obtain serum aminotransferase, alkaline phosphatase, and total bilirubin levels before initiation and during treatment as clinically indicated. Discontinue QUORVANTA if clinically significant liver injury is suspected. `[SAF-017, MON-002, MON-004, SAF-018]`

**Flushing:** QUORVANTA may cause flushing. In clinical trials of dimethyl tavorate, 34% of treated patients experienced flushing, compared with 5% on placebo; flushing generally began soon after initiation and usually improved or resolved over time. Taking QUORVANTA with food, or non-enteric coated aspirin (up to 325 mg) 30 minutes before dosing, may reduce the incidence or severity of flushing. `[SAF-019, SAF-020, ADM-004, DOS-005]`

**Serious gastrointestinal reactions:** Serious gastrointestinal reactions, including perforation, ulceration, hemorrhage, and obstruction, some with fatal outcomes, have been reported in the postmarketing setting with tavoric acid esters, including QUORVANTA, with or without concomitant aspirin. The majority occurred within 6 months of initiation. Monitor patients, promptly evaluate, and discontinue QUORVANTA for new or worsening severe gastrointestinal signs and symptoms. `[SAF-022, SAF-024]`

**Photosensitivity:** Photosensitivity reactions occurred in 4% of patients treated with dimethyl tavorate and 1% of placebo patients. Advise patients to limit exposure to sunlight and ultraviolet light and to use sun protection. `[SAF-025, SAF-026]`

**Most common adverse reactions** (incidence for dimethyl tavorate ≥10% and ≥2% more than placebo): flushing, abdominal pain, diarrhea, nausea, and headache. `[ADR-001]`

**Pregnancy:** Based on animal data, QUORVANTA may cause fetal harm. There are no adequate data on the developmental risk in pregnant women. A pregnancy exposure registry is available (1-800-555-0177). `[POP-001, POP-004]`

**Renal impairment:** QUORVANTA is not recommended in patients with moderate or severe renal impairment. `[DOS-007]`

*To report SUSPECTED ADVERSE REACTIONS, contact Arden Quay Biosciences Drug Safety at 1-800-555-0142 or FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088.* `[ADR-008]`

**Indication:** QUORVANTA is indicated for the treatment of relapsing forms of Brennick syndrome, to include first clinical episode, relapsing-remitting disease, and active progressive disease, in adults. `[IND-001]`
