**What is QUORVANTA?**

QUORVANTA is a prescription medicine for adults with relapsing forms of Brennick syndrome. `[DTC-001]`

QUORVANTA is taken by mouth, twice a day. `[DTC-002]`

QUORVANTA turns into the same active substance as dimethyl tavorate, a medicine that has been studied in people with relapsing Brennick syndrome for more than 10 years. `[DTC-004]`

### Study results (`/study-results`)

*What is QUORVANTA? | Study results | Taking QUORVANTA | Side effects | Savings and support | Important Safety Information*

**What the studies showed**

QUORVANTA turns into the same active substance as dimethyl tavorate, a medicine that has been studied in people with relapsing Brennick syndrome for more than 10 years. `[DTC-004]`

In two 2-year studies, people taking dimethyl tavorate had fewer relapses than people taking placebo. `[DTC-005]`

In one of those studies, people taking dimethyl tavorate were less likely to have their disability get worse than people taking placebo. In the other study, the difference was not statistically significant. `[DTC-006]`

People taking dimethyl tavorate in the studies had fewer new areas of damage on MRI than people taking placebo. `[DTC-007]`

### Taking QUORVANTA (`/taking-quorvanta`)

*What is QUORVANTA? | Study results | Taking QUORVANTA | Side effects | Savings and support | Important Safety Information*

**Taking QUORVANTA**

QUORVANTA is taken by mouth, twice a day. `[DTC-002]`

You can take QUORVANTA with or without food. `[DTC-011]`

Your healthcare provider will check your blood before you start QUORVANTA, 3 months after you start, and every 6 months after that. `[DTC-013]`

### Side effects (`/side-effects`)

*What is QUORVANTA? | Study results | Taking QUORVANTA | Side effects | Savings and support | Important Safety Information*

**Side effects**

The most common side effects were flushing, stomach pain, diarrhea, nausea and headache. `[DTC-008]`

Flushing and stomach problems were most common in the first months and usually got better over time. `[DTC-009]`

QUORVANTA can cause serious side effects, including allergic reactions, a rare brain infection called PML, shingles and other infections, low white blood cell counts, liver problems, serious stomach and bowel problems, and skin reactions to sunlight. `[DTC-012]`
